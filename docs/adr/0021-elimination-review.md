# Keep elimination review-only at every apply threshold

A surviving roster candidate may still be the wrong speaker. Carry elimination provenance through merging,
agreement boosts, rendering, bulk confirmation and seed selection. Cap its score below the configured apply
threshold and offer a manual alternative. At a zero threshold return a question that cannot be applied. A fixed
confidence such as 0.95 would turn absence of an alternative into an automatic name.
