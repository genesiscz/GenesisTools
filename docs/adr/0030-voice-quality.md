# Score voice outliers against independent compatible samples

Compare normalized prints within owner, seat, track, model and dimension. Score a sample against the centroid of
the other independent clips, with at least three compatible samples in the group. Report insufficient evidence
for a zero centroid or missing comparison. A score below the default 0.5 flags a clip to hear. Automatic removal
would treat a similarity score as a human rejection.
