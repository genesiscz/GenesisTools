# Ask the owner to identify shared room devices

Use explicitly mapped microphone groups now. After room R4, the proposed people-book mapping would let the owner
classify a roster account as a device. Offer that mapping after the account joins from the room's microphone
group in at least two meetings. A confirmed mapping attributes mute to the device and offers its human
occupants. Inferring the role automatically could hide a first-time guest.
